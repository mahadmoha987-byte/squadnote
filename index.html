Brief, qualified summaries appear during Competitive agent select only when enough players have contributed. Feedback describes reported tendencies, not facts about a person.

THE FLOW

After the match.
Before the next one.

The planned experience, from account linking to the summary you see.

  1. 01

    Link your account with Riot Sign On

    Sign in through Riot to verify your account. Squadnote does not ask for your Riot password. Squadnote will follow Riot’s guidance on player opt-in and data sharing.

  2. 02

    Choose tags after a Competitive match

    Select predefined tags about your experience with teammates. Shared feedback uses tags only: no free-text reviews, comments or public notes.

  3. 03

    Wait for multiple reports

    A single review does not create a public summary. Feedback stays unavailable until a minimum review threshold is met. The threshold will be published before launch.

  4. 04

    Read a short summary in agent select

    In Competitive agent select, the overlay shows aggregated, qualified tendencies such as “players often say…”. Summaries describe tendencies reported by the community, both strengths and frustrating habits, worded as reported patterns and never as verdicts. Individual reviews are never shown, there is no opponent scouting, and the overlay does not provide live tactical advice.

Privacy & removal

You should have a way to leave, and a way to raise a concern.

This website

This is a static information page. It has no sign-in form, analytics scripts, tracking cookies or player-data collection. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process connection information under GitHub’s privacy statement.

Deletion and removal requests

You can request deletion of your Squadnote account and associated data, or ask for a feedback summary to be removed or reviewed. During development, contact the maintainer through the project’s request channel below.

Start with a general request only. Do not post your Riot ID, email, account identifiers or other personal information publicly. The maintainer will arrange any necessary verification privately before handling account-specific data.
